The Joan Ganz Cooney Center is tracking various companies designing educational games; exploring their design processes, lessons learned, funding woes, strategic business decisions, and research findings. This initiative is featured on Games and Learning.org. We are grateful for the experience of sharing our story and the opportunity to give a glimpse into our background, process and experiences in the journey so far.
